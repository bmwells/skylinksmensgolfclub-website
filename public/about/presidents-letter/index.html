President's Letter – December 2025

Dear Skylinks Men's Club Members,

As we step into December, I hope this message finds you in good spirits and ready to embrace the final month of the year. It's hard to believe we're already gearing up for the last tournament of 2025! This year has been both quiet and exhilarating, particularly with the transition we've experienced within the board. I would like to take this opportunity to express my heartfelt gratitude to all the board members for their hard work, dedication, and commitment. A special thanks goes out to those who will be continuing their roles into 2026—you are truly appreciated!

Reflecting on the year, we've certainly experienced our share of peaks and valleys. From exciting tournaments to new initiatives, there's been a lot to celebrate. While we faced challenges—including the unfortunate cancellation of last month's tournament due to inclement weather, I firmly believe such obstacles only set the stage for an even more rewarding year ahead. For everyone who signed up for that November tournament, your entry fees will be seamlessly carried over to the January tournament, so there's no need to worry! Thank you for your understanding and continued support.

As we look ahead, I want to remind everyone that the deadline to sign up for our much-anticipated Christmas tournament is December 3rd. Following the sign-up, we will have our tournament tee assignment party on December 10th, where we can finalize the details and share in the camaraderie that makes our club so special. Mark your calendars, as this occasion will not only feature our meeting but also a holiday party filled with festive food and enjoyable company.

Additionally, on December 14th, we will host our final tournament of the year. This event is designed to be more than just a competition; it will include a delightful luncheon, exciting giveaways, and the presentation of trophies to celebrate our achievements throughout the year. It's an excellent opportunity to reflect on the bonds we've created and the challenges we've overcome together on the course.

I also want to take a moment to recognize our most improved golfers for this year, showcasing the talent and dedication within our ranks:

  • First Quarter: Leo Corrales
  • Second Quarter: Colin Holmes
  • Third Quarter: Jeff Mort

Stay tuned for the announcement of the most improved golfer for the fourth quarter, along with the overall most improved golfer of the year, which will be featured in January's president's letter. It's always inspiring to see the growth among our members!

Looking ahead, I want to assure everyone that we are diligently working on finalizing next year's tournament schedule. We aim to have it available on our website as soon as possible, which will allow everyone ample time to plan and decide on the tournaments they would like to participate in. Your participation in those tournaments has been invaluable, and I hope you had as much fun competing as we did organize the events.

In closing, I want to express my sincerest gratitude to each and every one of you for making this year a memorable one. Your enthusiasm, sportsmanship, and camaraderie have truly brought the Skylinks Men's Club to life. I am excited to see you all out on the course for the final tournament and the festivities surrounding it.

Wishing you all a wonderful holiday season filled with joy and laughter!

Ted Lewandowski
President, Skylinks Men's Golf Club

President's Letter – May 2025

Welcome to May!

As we step into the month of May, it's a great opportunity to reflect on how the first four months of this year have flown by. Time seems to have passed quickly, and as we look back, I want to express my heartfelt appreciation to everyone in the club for your continued support, patience, and understanding as we have transitioned from the old board to the new board. Your engagement and positive attitudes have made this process much smoother, and it truly embodies the spirit of our community.

I'd like to take a moment to recognize the hard work and dedication of the new board members. Each of you has committed your valuable time and effort over these past few months, and I am grateful for the incredible job you've done. The level of commitment you've shown has likely exceeded what we all anticipated at the outset. With the successful completion of our first four tournaments, we've created a strong foundation for the rest of the year, and I'm excited about what's ahead!

I'm thrilled to announce that the club has awarded over 15,000 points to our members thus far. This number is bound to grow as we continue to host more tournaments and engage more participants throughout the year. These points contribute to enhancing the competitive spirit within our club and are a testament to the enthusiasm of our members. I would like to emphasize that this impressive total does not even account for the contributions from our ongoing 50-50 raffle.

Now, let's take a moment to celebrate our tournament winners! I want to extend my congratulations to all those who participated in our tournaments this year. Your participation not only enhances the competition but also strengthens the camaraderie among us. Thank you all for upholding the values of the club – sportsmanship, respect, and enjoyment of the game – which truly embody our mission.

We have seen some thrilling tournaments unfold this year. We kicked things off in January with the Kickoff Tournament, which sparked a great deal of enthusiasm amongst the members. The Past Presidents Tournament followed, and it was fantastic to hear so much positive feedback about it. A special thank you goes out to those who helped organize this event, ensuring everything goes smoothly.

We also introduced a new format with the 4-Man Memorial Tournament, which added a refreshing twist to our competitive line-up. I had the pleasure of participating in this event last month, and I can confidently say it was a lot of fun for everyone involved!

As we move forward, I would like to highlight the Mayor's Cup Match Play Qualifier. Congratulations to all 64 players advancing to the next round! I encourage you to enjoy this unique format, embrace the competition, and best of luck to each of you. I'm eager to hear about the exciting matches and stories that emerge from this tournament. The camaraderie and friendly rivalries that develop during match play are what truly make these events special.

I also want to extend a heartfelt thank you to everyone who volunteered their time to participate in the Quad Tournament held in mid-April. Despite facing less-than-ideal weather conditions, your commitment to the game was nothing short of inspiring. It takes great resilience and passion to compete under those circumstances, and you all demonstrate an unwavering spirit. Although we fell short in the competition, I am optimistic about our chances next year, and I know we can come back even stronger!

Finally, I would like to reiterate my appreciation to all club members and the executive board for your incredible dedication and support. Your efforts play a significant role in making our club an enjoyable and enriching environment for all members. I look forward to seeing you out on the golf course soon, sharing stories and experiences as we continue this wonderful season together.

As we move forward, I encourage everyone to engage in our upcoming activities. Additionally, I'm excited to remind everyone about our new initiative: any member who attends our meetings will receive a chance to win a $25 gift card as a token of our appreciation for your involvement. It's our way of saying thank you for your commitment to the club.

Here's to a great May ahead and many exciting tournaments to come!

Ted Lewandowski
Skylinks Men's Club President
